macOS says Skeepto.app is damaged and should be moved to the Trash.
The file is intact. The system says that because this build is not
notarized by Apple.

Click Cancel. Do not move the app to the Trash.

Copy Skeepto.app to the Applications folder, then paste this in Terminal:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Skeepto.app
open /Applications/Skeepto.app

Or, after clicking Cancel once: System Settings, Privacy & Security,
then Open Anyway.
